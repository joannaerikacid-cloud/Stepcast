The Basics

What is Stepcast
and how does it work?

Stepcast is a self-guided audio walking tour you access on your phone browser. No app to download, no account needed and no time slot to book. Each tour covers a curated route through a city, with narrated audio and written text at every stop covering history, architecture, culture and the contemporary city.
No. Stepcast works entirely in your phone browser. Open the link, unlock the tour and start walking. Works on iPhone and Android, any browser, with no download and no account required.
Stepcast tours are created by Erika, a Clinical Specialist and passionate traveller based in the UK. Every city is personally visited before a tour is written. Each stop is thoroughly researched, covering history, architecture, culture and the contemporary city. The research goes beyond the obvious, drawing on local sources and first-hand visits to get under the skin of each city.
No app, no account, no subscription. Every tour is personally visited and researched, not written from a desk. The first 3 stops are always free so you can judge the quality before paying. And at €4.99 for lifetime access, there are no hidden costs.

of your walk.

That is exactly why Stepcast exists. Most group tours start at a fixed time, often 10am, and if your flight lands at noon or you want a slow morning, you have missed it. With Stepcast, you start when you are ready. No schedule, no rushing.

Pricing & Access

how to unlock.

Each tour costs €4.99 to unlock the full route. The first 3 stops are always free on every tour, so you can try before you buy. One payment, lifetime access. No subscription, no recurring charges.

Your unlock code is sent by email after purchase. If you have lost it, check your inbox or spam folder for an email from Lemon Squeezy. You can also contact us via the contact page and we will help you retrieve it.
